# About AI News Daily

AI News Daily is an automatically curated feed of AI news, organized into five topics:

- **Regulation** — US/global AI policy, government actions, legislation, export controls
- **Companies** — funding, model releases, stock impact, executive moves
- **Papers** — ArXiv, GitHub trending, Hugging Face models
- **Robotics** — humanoid robots (Figure, 1X, Tesla Optimus, Boston Dynamics), industrial automation, autonomous vehicles, drones, surgical/medical robots
- **News** — general breakthroughs, societal impact, meta-trends

Built with [Astro](https://astro.build/), deployed on [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/). Source: [github.com/Kelboto/ai-news-website](https://github.com/Kelboto/ai-news-website).
